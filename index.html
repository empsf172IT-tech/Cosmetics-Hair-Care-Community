The Science of Surfactants: Why Sulfate-Conscious Matters

Sulfates (such as Sodium Lauryl Sulfate) are efficient cleansing agents, but their high negative charge density can strip away the hair fiber’s natural 18-MEA protective lipid layer. For curly, coily, colored, or chemically processed strands, this leads to elevated cuticle scales, hygral fatigue, and color fading.

* Formulation Nuance: Sulfates are not inherently toxic, but milder coconut-derived isethionates and glucosides provide gentle micellar cleansing that preserves moisture balance without compromising hygiene.
